SHOPEASE E-COMMERCE - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

=== ORDER & SHIPPING ===
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days.
Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and costs $9.99.
Orders above $50 qualify for free standard shipping.
You can track your order using the tracking link sent to your email after dispatch.
Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, cancellation is not guaranteed.

=== REFUND & RETURNS ===
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery.
Items must be unused and in original packaging to qualify for a return.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.
To initiate a return, go to My Orders and click Return Item.

=== BILLING & PAYMENTS ===
We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and UPI.
Payments are encrypted and processed securely.
If you were charged but order was not placed, the amount will be refunded within 3-5 business days.
You can save multiple payment methods in your account settings.

=== ACCOUNT & PASSWORD ===
To reset your password, click Forgot Password on the login page.
A reset link will be sent to your registered email address.
If you cannot access your email, contact support for identity verification.
You can update your email, phone, and address from Account Settings.

=== PRODUCT QUESTIONS ===
All products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty unless stated otherwise.
Product specifications are listed on each product page.
If a product arrives damaged, contact support within 48 hours with photos.

=== COMPLAINT & ESCALATION ===
For unresolved issues, you can request to speak with a senior support agent.
Complaints are reviewed within 24-48 hours.
You can submit a formal complaint via the Help Center under Contact Us.
